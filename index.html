About Lawncraft

Lawncraft is for the homeowner with the patchy yard and the full-time job — the one who won't pay a lawn service $400 a season but will absolutely spend a Saturday fixing it himself if someone hands him a straight plan. Our rule is simple: every guide shows the cost, the timeline, and the DIY-vs-hiring-out math. If a job is genuinely better hired out, we'll say so.

No miracle products, no garden-center upsells. Just practical lawn care: revival and repair, mowing and maintenance schedules, weed and pest identification and treatment, and honest buying guides for seed, fertilizer, and equipment.
